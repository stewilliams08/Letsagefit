Questions? We've got you.

Before you decide.

Is this safe for me if I have joint pain or health concerns?

Yes. Everything starts with a conversation about your body, your history, and any limitations. Your coach builds the plan around what's safe for you and adjusts as you go — nothing is one-size-fits-all.

I haven't exercised in years. Is it too late to start?

It's never too late — and starting later is exactly who this is for. We begin gently, meet you at your level, and build from there. Most members are surprised how quickly they feel steadier and stronger.

Will I be the oldest or least-fit person there?

No. Our members are adults 55+ at every starting point, and the room is welcoming and unhurried — no judgment, and no pressure to keep up with anyone else. Your training starts with where you are today. Just people working on staying strong and independent.

Do I need any equipment or experience?

None. Just comfortable clothes and shoes. Your coach guides every movement and shows you exactly what to do.

What does it cost?

Your first consultation and movement assessment are free — no pressure, no commitment. We'll talk through the options and the right plan for your goals when you come in.
